About Us

Your Trusted Partner in Professional Cleaning

Company Introduction

At FJA Professional Hood Cleaning and Pressure Washing, we are committed to providing reliable, safe, and high-quality cleaning solutions for restaurants, commercial kitchens, businesses, and residential properties throughout Utah.

We specialize in kitchen hood and exhaust system cleaning, pressure washing, and deep cleaning services, ensuring your spaces remain hygienic, compliant, and looking their best.


Discover Our Services

Our Mission

To provide comprehensive, efficient, safe, and high-quality professional cleaning solutions that exceed our clients' expectations and contribute to the well-being, hygiene, and positive image of their spaces. We specialize in serving the needs of the food service and hospitality sectors, particularly restaurants and commercial kitchens, while also offering personalized service to the general public.

Our Vision

To become a leading professional cleaning company in the United States, recognized for the quality of our services, environmental responsibility, and commitment to total customer satisfaction. We currently operate in Utah, and our goal is to expand our operations to other states in the near future, bringing our standards of cleanliness and professionalism to more communities.
